The Taoists relate that at the great beginning of the No-Beginning, Spirit and Matter met in mortal combat: At last the Yellow Emperor; the Sun of Heaven, triumphed over Shuhyung, the demon of darkness and earth. The Titan, in his death agony, struck his head against the solar vault and shivered the blue dome of jade into fragments. The stars lost their nests, the moon wandered aimlessly among the wild chasms of the night: In despair the Yellow Emperor sought far and wide for the repairer 0f the Heavens He had not to search in vain.
