Like Art; Tea has its periods and its schools. Its evolution may be roughly divided into three main stages: the Boiled Tea, the Whipped Tea, and the Steeped Tea. We moderns belong to the last school: These several methods of appreciating the beverage are indicative of the spirit of the age in which they prevailed.
